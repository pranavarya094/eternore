Dear Aurora,

Eternore isn't a real word. It's two words folded into one: eterno, eternal, and amore, love.

This website is my way of making that literal, not just poetic. It will stay exactly as it is, right here on the internet, for as long as I keep it alive, and the only way it ever goes dark is if I stop renewing it. The only reason I would ever stop is if I am no longer breathing.

So however long that turns out to be, until my very last breath, I will keep writing for you here.

always, Pranav
