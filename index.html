For Aurora

a love written to outlast time
Dear Aurora,

Eternore isn't a real word. It's two words folded into one: eterno, eternal, and amore, love.

This website is my way of making that literal, not just poetic. It will stay exactly as it is, right here, for as long as I keep it alive.

So however long that turns out to be, until my very last breath, I will keep writing for you here.

always, Pranav

III.
Where it began

How It Began

It was an ordinary street on an ordinary day — or so I thought. Then you walked into it, and the ordinary world quietly ceased to exist.

I don't think either of us knew, in that first moment, what had just happened. But something had. A door had opened. And I have never once wished it closed.

You are my Catwoman — clever, fearless, a little unpredictable, and entirely impossible to forget. I am your Batman — stubborn, devoted, always finding my way back to you no matter how dark the night gets. We are a strange and perfect pair.

Life will bring us its highs and its lows. There will be days that shimmer, and days that are harder to carry. But through all of it, I want you to know this: I will always reach for your hand.

Happy Valentine's Day, Aurora.

— Pranav Arya
